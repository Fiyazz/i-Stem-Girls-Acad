### <u>WEEK 3: PowerPoint & Storytelling</u>

### This Week You’ll Learn:

- How to make creative presentations.
- How to organize information clearly.
- How storytelling helps make ideas more interesting.
### Activity 1 — My Favorite App Map

### Instructions:

- Pick your favorite app.
- Write down what it does, what you like about it, and what could be improved.
- Create a mind map or short paragraph describing how it works. **Reflection: What makes a good app experience?**

### Activity 2 — My PowerPoint Presentation

### Instructions:

- Create a 5-slide presentation.
- Slide 1: Title Slide
- Slides 2–4: Main ideas and images
- Slide 5: Summary
- Add transitions and animations. **Reflection: What did you enjoy about making your presentation?**
### <u>WEEK 4: Hackathon Week</u>

### This Week You’ll Learn:

- How to work in a team to solve problems.
- How to create and present new ideas.
- How to communicate your project confidently. **Activity 1 — Presentation Polishing** **Instructions:**
- Review your PowerPoint slides.
- Add animations or improve your visuals.
- Practice your presentation with your team. **Reflection: How did teamwork help make your presentation better?**

### Activity 2 — Creativity Hackathon

### Instructions:

- Work in a team to brainstorm a problem in your school or community.
- Create a 3-slide idea pitch using PowerPoint.
- Present your idea to your class. **Reflection: What was your team’s best idea, and why?**
### <u>End of Month Reflection</u>

**What was your favorite activity this month?** **What new skills did you learn about technology?** **How has your confidence in using tech tools grown?**
